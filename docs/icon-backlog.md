## Needs an official source

Simple Icons does not include Microsoft (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, Power BI, Clarity, Bing Webmaster Tools), Tableau, Ahrefs, Slack, SendGrid or Screaming Frog. Download the official logo from each vendor's brand or press kit, check its usage rules, then add it by hand following CONTRIBUTING.md.
